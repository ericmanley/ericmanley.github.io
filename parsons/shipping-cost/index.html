Shipping Cost

Shipping a package that weighs more than 10 lbs costs $18. If it weighs between 5 and 10 lbs, it costs $12. If it’s between 2 and 5 lbs, it costs $8. And, if it is less than 2 lbs, it costs $5. This program should determine the correct shipping cost. There may be distractor lines that are not part of the correct solution.
